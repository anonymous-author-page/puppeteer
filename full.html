TL;DR: 4Director uses complete object meshes and rigid trajectories in a shared 3D space to jointly control the camera, existing objects, and inserted objects in video world models.

Abstract

Precise control over camera and object motion is essential for professional video production. Existing video generation methods typically support either camera control alone or coarse object control in the image plane or in a 3D blob, which introduces ambiguity in depth and rotation and often fails to remain consistent across viewpoint changes. We introduce 4Director, a video world model conditioned on an explicit 4D scene representation comprising complete object geometry and per-frame rigid transformations. Each object is reconstructed once as a canonical mesh and animated through its prescribed transformations. This representation provides an intuitive 3D control interface and prevents unobserved geometry from being regenerated independently in every frame. We render the controlled scene as a depth video and introduce a Motion Adapter that transforms this geometric scaffold into video while synthesizing view-consistent appearance, illumination, and non-rigid dynamics. We further introduce Identity-Gated IoU (IG-IoU), which jointly evaluates adherence to prescribed object motion and preservation of object identity. Experiments demonstrate that 4Director achieves higher visual quality than prior methods while more faithfully realizing both camera and object controls.
