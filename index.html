TL;DR: Puppeteer directs camera motion, rigid object trajectories, and object insertion through an editable 4D Scene Puppet.

Abstract

Directing a video world model requires control over the camera and every object. Puppeteer is driven by the Scene Puppet, an editable 4D representation in which canonical objects, a background point cloud, and the camera share one 3D space. Users edit rigid object and camera trajectories, optionally insert an object reconstructed from a reference image, and render the resulting puppet as depth control. A Puppet Motion Adapter turns this rigid control into video with view-consistent appearance, illumination, and non-rigid motion.
